SCM guide

Supplementary cementitious materials replace part of the Portland cement. The mix table above assumes the replacement is by binder mass, and the water/cement ratio stays based on the total binder.

Replacements above the listed range need a trial batch: early strength slows down, so formwork stripping times have to move with it. Carbon savings use 0.83 kg CO₂e per kg of cement replaced.
